# Good Luck Cap Promo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Quiet athletic product film for Good Luck Running: documentary product photography meets a precise apparel lookbook. The cap remains the hero in every frame. Motion is confident and clean, with short electric-blue wipes connecting black, white, and cool-gray scenes. The overall feeling is practical, energetic, and premium rather than glossy or theatrical.

## Colors

- Ink: `#0A0A0C` - primary dark canvas and text panels
- Soft white: `#F7F8F5` - light canvas and primary text
- Cool gray: `#D9DCE2` - dividers and secondary details
- Good Luck blue: `#1677FF` - transition and metadata accent
- Carbon: `#24262B` - translucent photo overlays

## Typography

- Display and Traditional Chinese: `PingFang TC`, weight 900
- Metadata and technical labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, weight 500

## Motion

- Product images use restrained pans, scale pushes, and one subtle rotation.
- Scene changes use fast solid-blue or off-white wipes.
- Headlines enter with confident expo or power easing; metadata follows with lighter motion.
- No infinite animation and no decorative motion that competes with the cap.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use purple gradients, neon glow, floating cards, or bokeh decoration.
- Do not hide the cap under large text or dark blur.
- Do not invent technical performance claims.
- Do not add third-party logos or alter the embroidery shown in the source images.
- Do not use small web-sized typography.
